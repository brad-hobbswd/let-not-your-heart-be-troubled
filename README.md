## About This Story

Joey has always loved deeply.

Friendship mattered. Connection mattered. The people in his life became part of his heart, and somewhere along the way, that love slowly became heavy. Fear of distance, fear of change, and fear of losing people quietly settled into places he barely recognized.

Life kept moving.

Friends entered new seasons.

Relationships shifted.

Questions became louder than peace.

Joey found himself carrying emotional weight that was never meant to rest on his shoulders.

Through conversations, moments of prayer, difficult seasons, friendships, and encounters with Jesus, Joey slowly begins discovering a truth that changes everything.

Peace does not come from control.

Peace does not come from holding tighter.

Peace comes through surrender.

This journey follows Joey through fear, friendship, trust, healing, and the gentle process of learning that Jesus still holds every person and every burden safely in His hands.

This story was written for hearts that love deeply, carry quietly, and need the reminder that Jesus never asked anyone to hold the whole world together.

## About the Author

Bradley Hobbs writes from a place of faith, friendship, compassion, and hope.

Behind Joey's story stands the heart of a true friend. The kind of friend who chooses the high road. The kind who continues reaching during difficult seasons. The kind who keeps a hand extended even when another person struggles to receive help.

Some friendships walk beside people.

Some friendships reflect Jesus.
