Introduction

Let Not Your Heart Be Troubled
Joey’s Journey
Bradley Hobbs

“Peace is not found by holding tighter. Peace is found by trusting deeper.”

Life has a way of placing unexpected weight upon the heart. Relationships change. People move in different directions. Seasons shift. Distance grows. Questions appear where certainty once lived. Many people know what it feels like to hold tightly to those they love while silently carrying the fear of losing connection.

Joey's journey begins in a place many hearts understand. Deep care for people slowly becomes emotional exhaustion. Love becomes mixed with fear. The desire to remain close begins turning into pressure, anxiety, and an overwhelming need to keep everything together.

Through friendships, difficult emotions, unexpected moments, and encounters with the presence of Jesus, Joey begins discovering a truth that changes everything. Peace does not come from controlling relationships. Peace comes from surrendering hearts, fears, and loved ones into the hands of Jesus.

This story was written for every person who has carried burdens longer than necessary. Every person who has feared distance, change, or loss. Every person who has felt emotionally tired from trying to hold everything together.

Throughout these pages, one Scripture remains close:

John 14:1

"Let not your heart be troubled: ye believe in God, believe also in me."

Jesus never promised a life without change. Jesus promised His presence in every season. Joey's journey is not merely a story about friendship. Joey's journey is about learning to rest, surrender, trust, and discover that true peace is found in Him.

May these pages gently remind your heart of one truth:

Let not your heart be troubled.
