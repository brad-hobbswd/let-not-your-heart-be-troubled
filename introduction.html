Introduction

Let Not Your Heart Be Troubled
Joey's Journey
Bradley Hobbs

"Peace is not found by holding tighter. Peace is found by trusting deeper."

Life has a way of placing unexpected weight upon the heart. Relationships change. People move in different directions. Seasons shift. Distance grows. Questions appear where certainty once lived. Many people understand the feeling of holding tightly to those they love while quietly carrying fear.

Joey's story begins where many hearts have stood before. Deep care slowly becomes emotional exhaustion. Love becomes tangled with pressure, anxiety, and the exhausting need to keep everything together.

Friendships, difficult emotions, unexpected moments, and encounters with Jesus begin revealing a truth that changes everything. Peace does not come from controlling relationships. Peace grows through surrender.

These pages were written for those who have carried burdens too long, feared change, or quietly grown tired while trying to keep life from falling apart.

Throughout these pages, one Scripture remains close:

John 14:1

"Let not your heart be troubled: ye believe in God, believe also in me."

Jesus never promised a life without shifting seasons. Jesus promised His presence. Joey's journey explores rest, surrender, faith, and discovering peace found only in Him.

Let not your heart be troubled.
