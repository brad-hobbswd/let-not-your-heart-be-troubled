Welcome

Some hearts quietly carry more than anyone realizes. Joey cared deeply and slowly found himself overwhelmed by shifting seasons, uncertainty, and the fear of losing meaningful connections.

Unexpected moments, genuine friendships, and encounters with Jesus began revealing a different path. Rest slowly returned where pressure once lived.
