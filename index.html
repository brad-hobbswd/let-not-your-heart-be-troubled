About This Journey


Joey carried the weight of relationships, uncertainty, emotional exhaustion, and the quiet fear of losing people he deeply loved. Through friendships, prayer, difficult seasons, and encounters with Jesus, a powerful lesson begins unfolding.


Peace is not found by holding tighter. Peace is discovered through surrender, trust, and resting in the hands of Jesus.
