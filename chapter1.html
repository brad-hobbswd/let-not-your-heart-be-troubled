CHAPTER ONE

When the Heart Holds On

Joey sat on his small balcony in Kentucky, staring at the horizon as the late afternoon sun melted into streaks of orange and gold. He held his phone, scrolling through messages from friends scattered across states.

His heart was heavy.

Jeffery and Paul, living in Indiana, were excitedly planning a new business venture together. Noelle and Dallas had returned from a trip filled with stories. Anthony in Canada had shared excitement about reconnecting. Brad and Justin remained dependable, yet Joey felt a distance he could not explain.

“How did I get here?” Joey whispered.

He had always been the one holding tightly to everyone. Joey became the glue. Recently, emotional exhaustion quietly settled into his heart from trying to keep people close while life naturally carried everyone into different directions.

His phone buzzed.

Michelle.

"Joey, your heart is carrying too much. Sometimes letting go is not losing. Trust Jesus to hold them and hold you."

Joey remembered John 14:1.

"Let not your heart be troubled: ye believe in God, believe also in me."

Those words suddenly felt different.

Not circumstances.
Not people.
My heart.

Let not your heart be troubled.
