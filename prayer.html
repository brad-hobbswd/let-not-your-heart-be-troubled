Let Not Your Heart Be Troubled
Joey’s Journey
Bradley Hobbs

John 14:1

"Let not your heart be troubled: ye believe in God, believe also in me."

Many hearts carry silent fears. Worry sometimes settles quietly beneath relationships, responsibilities, and daily life. Joey discovered a lesson that transformed his heart. Peace never came through control. Peace arrived through surrender and trust in Jesus.

This prayer was written for anyone carrying emotional weight, fear of losing people, anxiety over changing seasons, or the burden of trying to hold everything together.

A Prayer of Trust

Jesus,

Today I place every burden, every relationship, every fear, and every troubled thought into Your hands. My heart grows tired from trying to carry responsibilities that were never mine to hold. My heart grows weary from trying to control circumstances and people I deeply love.

Your Word says in John 14:1, "Let not your heart be troubled: ye believe in God, believe also in me." Today I choose to believe You. Today I choose trust over fear and surrender over striving.

Please hold every person close to my heart. Watch over friends, family, and loved ones. Guard connections that matter and give me peace where distance or change appears.

Teach me to release worry and rest in Your presence. Teach me to love freely without fear. Teach me that surrender does not mean loss. Strengthen my faith so my heart remains anchored in You.

Thank You for Your faithfulness. Thank You for Your peace. Thank You for holding every part of my life securely in Your hands.

In the name of Jesus, Amen.

Peace grows stronger when hearts rest in Jesus.
