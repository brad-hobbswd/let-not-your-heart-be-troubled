About the Author

Let Not Your Heart Be Troubled
Joey’s Journey
Bradley Hobbs

"A real friend stays close enough to speak truth, wise enough to take the high road, and loving enough to keep a hand extended."

Bradley Hobbs writes with a heart centered on friendship, grace, and faith. Behind these pages stands more than a storyteller. Behind these words stands a friend.

This journey was written from the perspective of someone who deeply cares for Joey and genuinely wants the best for him. Real friendship does not disappear when life becomes difficult. Real friendship remains present through confusion, distance, frustration, and seasons when emotions become heavy.

Sometimes people travel paths that become painful. Sometimes hearts become troubled. Sometimes fear and uncertainty create moments where a person begins losing sight of peace. During those moments, a true friend does not walk away.

A true friend keeps reaching. A true friend keeps praying. A true friend keeps extending a hand even during moments when advice goes unheard and difficult choices continue unfolding.

This story carries a quiet reminder that friendship is not built upon control or conditions. Friendship sometimes means taking the high road. Friendship means choosing love over frustration and compassion over disappointment.

Joey's journey reflects more than emotional healing. Joey's journey reflects the kind of friendship that remains steady through storms. The kind that keeps believing, keeps caring, and keeps hoping for restoration.

Through every page rests a simple truth. Some people enter life for a season. Some remain through every storm. Those rare friendships become reminders of the way Jesus reaches for people with patience, mercy, and unwavering love.

Friendship reaches even when another hand struggles to reach back.
