About the Author

Let Not Your Heart Be Troubled
Joey's Journey
Bradley Hobbs

"A real friend stays close enough to speak truth, wise enough to take the high road, and loving enough to keep a hand extended."

Bradley Hobbs writes with a heart centered on friendship, grace, and faith. Behind these pages stands more than a storyteller. Behind these words stands a friend.

This journey was written from the perspective of someone who deeply cares for Joey and genuinely wants the best for him. Real friendship does not disappear when life becomes difficult. Real friendship remains present through confusion, distance, frustration, and seasons when emotions become heavy.

Sometimes people travel painful roads. Sometimes hearts become troubled. Sometimes fear and uncertainty create moments where peace becomes difficult to see.

A true friend keeps reaching. A true friend keeps praying. A true friend keeps extending a hand even when wisdom goes unheard and difficult choices continue unfolding.

Friendship is not built upon conditions. Friendship sometimes means taking the high road. Friendship means choosing compassion and remaining present through difficult seasons.

Joey's story reflects more than emotional healing. Joey's story reflects the kind of friendship that remains steady through storms. The kind that keeps believing, keeps caring, and keeps hoping.

Through every page rests a simple truth. Some people enter life for a season. Others remain through every storm. Those rare friendships become reminders of the way Jesus reaches with patience, mercy, and unwavering love.

A true friend keeps a hand extended, keeps believing, and keeps choosing the high road.
